When An Animal
Needs Help

This is how things usually happen today - Someone notices an injured animal. Messages go out on WhatsApp. Calls are made. Photos get forwarded. But the right person rarely sees it in time.

Slow Alerts

Messages take time to reach the right people.

Unclear Location

Volunteers may struggle to find the exact place.

Disconnected Volunteers

People willing to help often never see the alert.

Time Lost

Every minute without help can worsen injuries.

A Network
Of Nearby Volunteers

The Rapid Response App instantly broadcasts an alert to volunteers within the same district. Anyone nearby who is available can see the alert and coordinate help.

District Alert System

Alerts include the caller’s phone number, GPS location and a short description so nearby volunteers can respond quickly.

How The Network Works

01

Raise SOS

Open the app and press the SOS button when an animal needs urgent help.

02

Location Shared

The alert includes your phone number, GPS location and a short description.

03

District Alert

All Rapid Response users in the same district can see the alert.

04

Volunteer Responds

Nearby volunteers who are available can call and coordinate help.
