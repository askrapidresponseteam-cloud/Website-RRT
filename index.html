Animal Emergencies
Need Fast Coordination

When an animal is injured or in danger, help often depends on how quickly nearby people can coordinate and reach the location. Without a simple way to alert nearby volunteers, valuable time can be lost.

Delayed Coordination

People often rely on scattered phone calls, WhatsApp groups, or social media posts. These methods can take time and may not reach someone nearby quickly.

Location Confusion

When an emergency is reported without precise location details, responders may struggle to find the exact spot where help is needed.

A District-Level
Volunteer Network

The Rapid Response App allows anyone to quickly alert animal lovers and volunteers in the same district when an animal is injured, missing, or in distress.

emergency

Instant District Alerts

Once an SOS is raised, the alert becomes visible to all Rapid Response App users in the same district. Volunteers who see the alert and are able to help can contact the person who raised the SOS and coordinate assistance.

How The App Works

The Rapid Response App is a coordination network for animal emergencies. When someone raises an SOS alert, volunteers within the same district can see the alert and choose to respond if they are available.

01

Raise SOS

Open the app and press the SOS button if an animal needs urgent help.

02

Location Shared

The alert includes your phone number, the exact GPS location, and a short description of the situation.

03

District Alert

The SOS alert becomes visible to Rapid Response App users within the same district.

04

Volunteers Respond

Volunteers who are nearby and available may contact the person who raised the alert to coordinate help.

What The App Is & What It Is Not

What The App Is

  • check_circle A volunteer coordination network for animal emergencies.
  • check_circle A way to alert animal lovers and volunteers in the same district.
  • check_circle A tool that shares GPS location and contact information during emergencies.
  • check_circle A platform that allows volunteers nearby to respond if they are available.

What The App Is Not

  • cancel The app does not come with a rescue team.
  • cancel Raising an SOS does not guarantee help will arrive.
  • cancel The platform does not operate shelters or animal rescue services.
  • cancel The network is built by volunteers and grows stronger as more people join.

Who Uses
The Network

01 Volunteers

Individuals who are willing to help animals in distress and respond when an SOS alert appears in their district.

02 Animal Feeders

Community feeders who look after street animals and may need help during medical emergencies or difficult situations.

03 Pet Owners

Pet owners who may require urgent assistance if a pet goes missing, gets injured, or faces an unexpected emergency.

04 Animal Lovers

Anyone who cares about animals and wants to help when they see an alert in their district.
