A Network
Of Nearby Volunteers

The Rapid Response App instantly broadcasts an alert to volunteers within the same district. Anyone nearby who is available can see the alert and coordinate help.

notifications_active

District Alert System

Alerts include the caller’s phone number, GPS location and a short description so nearby volunteers can respond quickly.

How The Network Works

01

Raise SOS

Open the app and press the SOS button when an animal needs urgent help.

02

Location Shared

The alert includes your phone number, GPS location and a short description.

03

District Alert

All Rapid Response users in the same district can see the alert.

04

Volunteer Responds

Nearby volunteers who are available can call and coordinate help.

Why More Volunteers Matter

District With Many Volunteers

  • group Many people in the district have the app installed.
  • notifications The SOS alert reaches many nearby volunteers instantly.
  • call Someone nearby is likely to respond and coordinate help.

District With Few Volunteers

  • person_off Very few people in the district are using the app.
  • notifications_off The SOS alert may be seen by only a small number of volunteers.
  • schedule Help may take longer or may not arrive.

The Rapid Response App works best when more people in a district join the network. Every new volunteer increases the chances that an animal in distress receives help quickly.

What The App Is
& What It Is Not

What The App Is

  • check_circle A coordination network for animal emergencies.
  • check_circle A way to alert nearby volunteers in your district.
  • check_circle A tool that shares GPS location and contact number.
  • check_circle A platform where volunteers can respond if available.

What The App Is Not

  • cancel The app does not come with a rescue team.
  • cancel Raising an SOS does not guarantee help.
  • cancel The platform does not operate shelters.
  • cancel The app does not connect to a team that can directly rescue, transport, or relocate animals.
