Rapid Response Team · A peer to peer network for animal welfare

Do one bit.
Ask for the rest.

No control room, no staff, no helpline. Just the people near you who also have the app, and a fast way to reach them when an animal needs more hands than you have.

Get the app

First things first

Responder first: the one rule

RRT is not a reporting platform. Posting a problem is not helping an animal. Do one bit yourself, then ask the network to carry the next bit. That is the whole idea.

Your district

Your district is assigned the moment you sign up, from where you are standing. Open the app somewhere else and it moves with you. Nothing is tracked in between.

SOS and asks stay inside your district. A vet consult is the only thing that crosses district lines.

Your privacy

RRT is not a directory of phone numbers, on purpose.

You can still see how strong your district is: open the Chats tab and tap Responders. You get names, never numbers. A number appears in one place only, on a live SOS, to the people who might come.

What the app does

01SOS · Right now

Something is happening in front of you.

  • Dog hit by a car, needs a lift to a vet.
  • Cow down on the road, needs help to a facility.
  • A feeder being threatened, needs someone to stand with them.
  • Pup in a storm drain, needs two more hands.
  • Street dog in labour, needs a safe corner tonight.
  • Injured kite tangled in manja, needs a carrier.
  • Dog on a chain with no water, needs a neighbour to check.

Type a short brief. It reaches everyone in your district who has the app. They see your name, your number, where you are and what you wrote. Add photos and a voice note so they can decide whether they can help.

Only they see your number, never the other way round, and only while the SOS is on. It stops itself after 60 minutes if you have not.

02Ask the District · This week

Urgent, but not a siren.

  • Foster support for a week or two.
  • Who is genuinely the best vet in town.
  • Pet friendly apartments, restaurants, places to go.
  • Just moved from Jayanagar to Thane and know nobody here.
  • Blood donor needed for a surgery on Friday.
  • Putting together an ABC camp or a vaccination drive.

The same district, at a slower speed. Anyone nearby can answer, and their answer opens a private chat with you alone.

No phone numbers appear anywhere in Ask the District.

03Shop

Supertails, inside the app.

Order food, medicine and everything else for the animals you look after, without leaving RRT. The same prices as Supertails' own site, in a faster place to shop them.

You pay Supertails, on their own secure checkout. RRT holds no stock, takes no cut and never sees your card.

There is a vegetarian mode that hides anything it cannot confirm is vegetarian.

04Vet · Coming soon

A vet, without leaving the house.

An online consult for pet parents and for the people who look after community animals. Your request goes out to every vet online at that moment. It looks for ten minutes, and stops if nobody is free.

Chat, or video if they need to see the animal. No phone number is shown, not yours and not the vet's.

A vet may charge up to Rs 199 for their time, paid to them directly. RRT charges no fee of any kind, for any service, ever.
