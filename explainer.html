Rapid Response · A peer to peer network for animal welfare

Do one bit. Ask for the rest.

No control room, no staff, no helpline. Just the people near you who also have the app, when an animal needs more hands than you have.

Get the app
The one rulePosting a problem is not helping an animal. Do one bit yourself, then ask the network to carry the next bit.
Your districtAssigned from where you stand, moves with you. SOS and asks stay inside it; only a vet consult crosses district lines.
Names, never numbersRRT is not a phone directory. Your number appears in one place only: on your live SOS, to the people who might come.
Free, alwaysNo fee of any kind, for any service, ever. No ads, no tracking in between.

What the app does

01SOS · Right now

Something is happening in front of you.

Type a short brief, add photos or a voice note. Everyone in your district with the app sees it and can come help.

Dog hit by a carPup in a storm drainFeeder being threatened

Your number shows only while the SOS is on. It ends itself after 60 minutes.

02Ask the District · This week

Urgent, but not a siren.

The same district, at a slower speed. Anyone nearby can answer, and their answer opens a private chat with you alone.

Foster for two weeksBest vet in town?Blood donor on Friday

No phone numbers appear anywhere in Ask the District.

03Shop

Supertails, inside the app.

Food, medicine and more at Supertails' own prices, paid on their secure checkout. RRT holds no stock, takes no cut, never sees your card.

FoodMedicinesVegetarian mode

Vegetarian mode hides anything it cannot confirm is vegetarian.

04Vet · Coming soon

A vet, without leaving the house.

Your request goes to every vet online at that moment. Chat, or video if they need to see the animal. No numbers shown, either way.

Pet parentsCommunity animalsChat then video

A vet may charge up to Rs 199, paid to them directly. RRT charges nothing, ever.
