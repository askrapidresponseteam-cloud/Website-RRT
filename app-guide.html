02 RRT APP GUIDE
START HERE / THE BIG IDEA
Everything is
local.
Help has to be near. So the app only shows what is happening in your district - the local area your phone is in, like Udupi or Bangalore.
Like a local noticeboard: put up a notice or raise an alarm, and only people in your area see it. Travel somewhere new and it switches by itself. It even knows your district with no internet.

You see only your area

Alerts, asks and chats are all filtered to where you are.

It follows you

Move districts and everything re-points itself.

Two kinds of feature

Most are district-only. A few work anywhere.

Watch the SCOPE tag on every card. Red DISTRICT means your area only. Black ANYWHERE means not tied to your district.

20 RRT APP GUIDE
VETS / REPORT A FACILITY
Report a government
vet hospital.
If a government vet facility failed an animal - no doctor, no medicines, filthy - file a documented report the app delivers to the authority.
1
Name the facility (and a landmark).
2
Tick what's wrong - no doctor, no medicines, broken equipment, unhygienic, staff misconduct, building disrepair, other - and/or describe it.
3
Take a live photo (camera, stamped with date, time, GPS) and drop a pin at the facility.
4
Enter your email and send. You get a reference number.

The app makes a PDF and emails it to the authority and to you. Their replies go straight to your inbox. Works anywhere in the country.

For
Government vet hospitals
Goes to

YOUR PRIVACY / AT A GLANCE
What's kept,
and for how long.
The app holds as little about you as possible. Four promises:
1
Your number is private - shown only during an active SOS.
2
Chats are wiped nightly at midnight (India time).
3
Emergency photos and voice vanish when the SOS ends, or after 60 minutes.
4
You can delete everything from Profile, on phone and servers.

Location is never tracked in the background. Only when you raise an alert, refresh, or share a pin.
