Who runs this shop

Rapid Response designs and hosts this storefront. Everything on it is sold and delivered by Pets Lifestyle, the seller for every order placed here: the same store behind the SHOP tab in the RRT app.

Pets Lifestyle takes payment on their own secure checkout, holds the order, ships it, and handles returns, refunds and cancellations. We do not process payments and cannot access your payment details. Prices, stock and photos on this page are read live from their store.

For anything about an order, use the link in your confirmation email or see your orders.
